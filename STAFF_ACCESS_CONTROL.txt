AR LIBRARY - STAFF ACCESS CONTROL

Admin can create/edit staff accounts and grant module-wise access.

Available permissions:
- Dashboard
- Seats & Lockers
- Members
- Fees & Payments
- Attendance
- Attendance QR
- Enquiry
- Expenses
- Reports
- Backup & Restore
- Library Settings

Important:
1. Admin accounts always have full access.
2. Staff accounts can be given only the modules selected by Admin.
3. Access is enforced in both the sidebar and PHP/API endpoints.
4. "Clear All" is a real zero-permission state after the Admin saves it.
5. Existing staff accounts remain backward-compatible until an Admin saves a custom permission set.
6. The main Admin account STF-001 cannot be downgraded or deleted.
